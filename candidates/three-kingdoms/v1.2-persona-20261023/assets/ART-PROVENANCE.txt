THREE KINGDOMS: WHOLE-CANVAS LANDSCAPE, INTERNAL WAR-MAP, AND NINE FIGURES

Original historical-themed paintings created for this project using OpenAI image generation. No commercial-game art or third-party portrait was supplied or incorporated. Portraits are artistic interpretations, not authenticated likeness reconstructions. Source atlases and review proofs remain outside this runtime package.

Whole-canvas landscape is viewport-attached cover/center. The independently clipped war-map remains inside the actual central triangle and three frontier corridors. Nine transparent WebP cutouts use fixed crops and cleaned alpha edges; no painted text is embedded. Live SVG renders all board geometry, roles, figure names and titles.

The nine identities use representative career roles, not a same-year roster: 魏 曹操·主公, 荀彧·谋士 (汉侍中、尚书令), 司马懿·太傅; 蜀 刘备·主公, 诸葛亮·丞相, 庞统·谋士 (军师中郎将); 吴 孙权·主公, 周瑜·都督 (前部大督), 鲁肃·谋士 (赞军校尉; 后为横江将军). These identities are presentation-only and add no chess abilities. Historical source notes are in persona-roles.json.

Runtime motion is limited presentation animation of static layers: active-faction-only restrained movement and portrait crossfade. No skeletal animation or full-frame animated character footage is claimed.
